    About us

    Hiring is slow for companies and unfair for job seekers. We fix both.

    South African companies receive hundreds of CVs for one advert, and HR teams lose days reading them. At the same time, 8.5 million people are unemployed and young job seekers rarely hear back. HireSmart AI is an AI-powered recruitment and headhunting system that helps South African companies, especially small and medium businesses, find the right candidates for their vacancies faster and more easily. It screens and ranks every applicant with a clear, explainable score, searches our talent pool for strong candidates who did not apply, and keeps a human in charge of every hiring decision.

    Our aim

    To cut the time and cost of hiring for South African companies while giving every job seeker, wherever they live, a fair and free chance to be seen.

    Why it matters for communities

    Faster, cheaper hiring helps small businesses grow and create jobs. Free profiles and location-aware matching connect young people in townships and rural areas to work they would not otherwise find.

    What makes us different

    • Transparent scores. Candidates can ask why they scored as they did.
    • Rand pricing on the website. No "contact us for a quote".
    • Headhunting at 8%, below typical agency fees of 15% to 30%.
    • Data hosted in South Africa (AWS Cape Town region).
